Findings from the research pass, mapped to mechanism. Used to ground the letters below in real, sourced language rather than assumption.

**Armando / PrimeShift**
- Management-role employees report higher rates of job-related weight gain than non-management roles (49% vs. 43%, CareerBuilder/Harris Poll) — desk-bound, high-responsibility work is a documented risk factor, not a personal failing.

- Hispanic/Latino men have been found in peer-reviewed research to pursue more extreme weight-loss strategies than white men on average — suggesting Armando has likely already tried something drastic and failed, which supports a "system," not "willpower," reframe.

**Carolina / The Tortilla Method**
- Registered dietitians and academic focus-group studies confirm Latino food is routinely stigmatized by health professionals unfamiliar with the cuisine — this is a documented industry failure, not a personal insecurity.
- Only 6% of registered dietitians in the U.S. are Latino — Carolina has statistically likely never been coached by someone who understands her plate.
- For many Latino households, food functions as a culturally sanctioned stress response and even a folk remedy — meaning "cut the bread" isn't heard as diet advice, it's heard as being asked to give up a coping mechanism.
- GLP-1 drugs run roughly $250/week without insurance (~$3,000 for 90 days) with self-reported side effects in 43.5% of Reddit users studied — a real, costed, researchable alternative Carolina is actively weighing, not a hypothetical.
- Restrictive dieting has a documented physiological trap: fat loss lowers leptin, which increases appetite, and short-term diets see 30–65% of lost weight regained within a year. This gives the "system over restriction" positioning a real mechanism, not just a lifestyle preference.

And the old system — eat okay, walk sometimes, don't think about it too hard — was built for a body and a life that don't exist anymore. It's not that you stopped trying. It has travel weeks and normal weeks, high-stress quarters and slow ones, client dinners you can't skip and days that get eaten by back-to-back meetings.
